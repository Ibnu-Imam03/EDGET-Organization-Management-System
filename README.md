# EDGET Organization Management System

A software system designed to help manage EDGET, a student-led organization connected to Adama Science and Technology University (ASTU).

## About EDGET

EDGET student volunteers support children through Saturday teaching sessions and individual home teaching. The organization also coordinates family visits (Ziyara), fundraising, events, and other activities.

## Main Features

- **Organization Management:** Executives, members, sectors, and responsibilities.
- **Academic Management:** Children, families, teaching assignments, and teaching sessions.
- **Ziyara Management:** Family visits, team assignments, and visit reports.
- **Finance Management:** Income, expenses, and fundraising activities.
- **Event Management:** Welcome events, closing events, trips, and participation.
- **Information Management:** Documents, announcements, and meeting records.

## Organization Structure

1. Executive Leadership
2. Academic Sector
3. Ziyara Sector
4. Finance Sector
5. Event Sector
6. Information Sector
